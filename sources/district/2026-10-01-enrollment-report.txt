HUUSD Comprehensive Attending Student Data


The tables on the following pages include attending student data by grade and by school at the elementary schools, the middle schools, and the high school. 

Columns Explanation for All Schools


   * Active Resident Students: Currently enrolled students who live in the school’s city/town
   * IDC Out: Continuing: Residents of that school continuing IDC this year
   * IDC Out: New: Residents of that school choosing IDC for 26-27
   * IDC In: Continuing/New: Residents of other schools either continuing or new to IDC 26-27 at this school 
   * Attending Resident Students: The # of Resident students choosing to attend the resident school (and not choosing IDC)
   * Tuition In: Students who are tuitioning in to that school
   * Total Attending Students: Students attending HUUSD schools

Additional Columns Explanation for HUHS


   * Early College Out: Attending all classes off-site and not included in HUHS attending numbers
   * H.S. Public Choice In: Students who entered public high school choice lottery to attend HUHS
   * Dual Enrollment: 11th and 12th grade students who attend 1 or 2 classes off site and the rest on site; they are included in attending numbers
   * Total Attending Students: Includes Residents, High School Choice In, Tuition
