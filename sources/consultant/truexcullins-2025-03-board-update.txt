Process
Study Purpose: 
The purpose of this planning phase is provide the District with an understanding of the  long-term financial and educational impacts of district configuration options relative to each other, the status quo and District planning goals so as to identify several options that merit further investigation.

Steps taken to date: 
To develop planning guidelines, detailed questionnaires were sent to educational leadership teams for elementary, middle and high school levels
TruexCullins was asked to iterate multiple options that might optimize operational efficiency and educational effectiveness
15 planning options were identified and assessed relative to the planning guidelines
From those 15 options, the Facility Committee selected 6 for cost modeling.
Tonight’s goal:
The goal is for the School Board to select up to 3 options for further study (Facility Evaluation and Concept Design  Phase). This would include the development of floor plans and site plans for each affected site, and would include cost estimates.

Cost Modeling Summary
17

Findings
Bonding for projects saves substantial amounts vs budgeting on a yearly basis for capital renewal/deferred maintenance.
Less overall square footage, and fewer facilities of greater capacity yields greater long-term savings.
Achieving significant savings through reconfiguration requires investment in facilities
Achieving equity of space requires investment in facilities.

18

Final Thoughts
TruexCullins was asked to provide as many options as possible to facilitate a community conversation about what is best for students -  supported by data and analysis, and using the lens of economies of scale.
However, we recognize this a complex issue involving more than building plans and budgets
In addition to potential cost savings, there are many educational, social, and extracurricular benefits to consider for students.

Next Steps

The goal is for the School Board to select up to 3 options for further study at their next meeting. 

To proceed with further investigation, there are two phases ahead: Facility Evaluations of all district facilities, and Concept Design, which includes the development of floor plans and site plans for each affected site, and would include cost estimates.
