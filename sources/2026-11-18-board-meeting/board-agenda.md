6\. Discussion (90-120 minutes)

* [Potential School Closures](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1_EJhS_6iEnNCkJkcDHJja9nYSIw45Ds2KrlQ4g5nVE4/edit?slide=id.h404d8c50a21bce71_16_940#slide=id.h404d8c50a21bce71_16_940) – Board Deliberation  
  * Led by the Board Chair and Vice-Chair, this discussion will focus on whether one or more HUUSD schools should be considered for potential closure through the public hearing process outlined in the district's Articles of Agreement.  
  * Board members will participate in a structured discussion addressing three questions:  
    1. **What does the evidence tell us?** What are the most important findings from the school configuration ratings, building information, and recent facility walkthroughs?  
    2. **What are the tradeoffs?** What educational, operational, financial, and community considerations should influence the board's decisions?  
    3. **Where do we go from here?** Based on the information available, which school or schools, if any, should move forward for public hearings on potential closure, and why?  
  * *The purpose is to provide each board member an opportunity to share their perspective and explain their reasoning. **No motions or votes will occur during this discussion.** Any motions to formally consider specific schools for potential closure and schedule public hearings will be addressed separately under Action Items later in the meeting.*
