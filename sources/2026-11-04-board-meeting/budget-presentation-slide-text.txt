NOTES: Presenter notes — Grade Alignment |  | Definition: Grade alignment is the intentional organization of grade spans and teaching pathways across HUUSD, so students in the same grade have comparable access and coordinated experiences. The pathway shown is illustrative, not a final Board-approved placement plan. |  | Talking points: | 1. Student opportunity: A common grade structure can make it easier to schedule specialized instruction, enrichment, electives and peer experiences equitably, and coordinate teachers and student-support teams. | 2. Curriculum continuity: Alignment facilitates a shared curriculum sequence, common expectations and purposeful transition planning. | 3. Community and unified district identity: Families can plan around a clear pathway; students can make connections across HUUSD towns. Shared learning experiences can cultivate one HUUSD identity, a sense of districtwide pride, belonging, and responsibility for all students. This is additive: a unified district identity should celebrate, not erase, the distinct traditions and character of each town and school. However, community schools are centers of local identity and relationships. Families will rightly ask about school identity, travel time, after-school participation, siblings and traditions. Those concerns must be addressed in design and implementation, not dismissed. | 4. Important nuance: Grade configuration alone does not guarantee academic benefits; research is mixed and school-to-school transitions can create challenges without careful support. Present these as design goals, not proven outcomes. |  | Sources for background: University of Minnesota Center for Applied Research and Educational Improvement, Review of Literature on Grade Configuration and School Transitions (https://carei.umn.edu/review-literature-grade-configuration-and-school-transitions); NEASC, Curriculum Alignment, 2024 (https://www.neasc.org/sites/default/files/2024-01/neasc-cis%20-%20MANUAL%20school%20improvement%20-%20r2024-01_final.pdf); National Rural Education Association rural consolidation overview (https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ783851).
