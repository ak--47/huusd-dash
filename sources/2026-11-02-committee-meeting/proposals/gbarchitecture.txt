3. PROJECT UNDERSTANDING
We understand that the Harwood Uniﬁed Union School District (HUUSD) is seeking to facilitate a feasibility study which
looks to assess the poten4al for consolida4ng four elementary schools into one site at 193 Home Farm Way in Waitsﬁeld.
HUUSD has already assembled facility condi4on assessments of the four PK-6 elementary schools at Fayston, Moretown,
Waitsﬁeld, and Warren.

The poten4al site at Home Farm Way will be assessed for it’s ability to improve and strengthen educa4onal opportunity,
program delivery, opera4onal and staﬃng eﬃciency, and create a sustainable loca4on for a consolidated elementary
school for years to come. HUUSD has iden4ﬁed student learning, belonging, safety, and equitable access to programs as
central themes throughout the analysis. The results and process of the analysis must be clearly communicated to all com-
muni4es involved.

Though the assessment is focusing on poten4al consolida4on, this is not a forgone conclusion and the analysis will only be
used in future decision making only. The overall analysis must also compare a poten4al consolida4on with a con4nua4on
of opera4ng the exis4ng facili4es. Recommenda4ons and concepts must be inclusive of all opera4onal implica4ons, in-
cluding a lens of sustainability, in regards to maintenance, transporta4on, energy use, and environmental goals.

We understand that there are many challenges to planning a school, beyond what is inside the classroom and ac4vity
spaces. The site at which a school is located must be able to accommodate bus routes, parking, play area, along with nec-
essary u4li4es. All of these elements require space, and arranging these appropriately is a challenge at many sites.

feasibility study. gbA will review relevant background materials provided by the district including the existing campus and
