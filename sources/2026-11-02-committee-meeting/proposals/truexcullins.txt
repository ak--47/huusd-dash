Re: HUUSD Elementary Feasibility RFP


Dear Dr. Leichliter and Members of the Selection Committee,

Thank you for the opportunity to submit our proposal for the District Elementary Consolidation Feasibility Study.

For over a decade, TruexCullins has partnered with HUUSD on facility planning and improvements across the District.

Most recently, we worked together on district-wide master planning, exploring 15 configurations for improving
educational effectiveness and operational efficiency, including different approaches to consolidating the Valley
elementary schools. This was followed by detailed facility evaluations of six elementary and middle schools in 2025.

That work gives us a strong head start on this study. We already know the schools, their facility and site needs, the
reconfiguration options considered, and the assumptions behind that analysis. Rather than repeat that work, we can sit
down with Ray Daigle and District leadership, update what has changed, and focus on what HUUSD needs to know now:
whether 193 Home Farm Way offers a viable and beneficial long-term solution for the Valley elementary schools.

We also bring the perspective of helping other Vermont districts wrestle with similar decisions. At OCSU, district-wide
planning led to further study of a consolidated campus. At Taconic & Green, options continue to evolve as costs and
community priorities are tested. At Mount Abraham, the Board ultimately halted further reconfiguration study following
strong public opposition to potential school closures. Different outcomes, but the same goal: give school leaders and
communities the information they need to make consequential facility decisions with confidence and adjust course as
circumstances evolve.

We know HUUSD, we know the work that brought the District to this point, and we understand the importance of
getting the next step right. We would welcome the opportunity to continue our partnership.

Sincerely,

     ABOUT TRUEXCULLINS
      Educational Planning, Design, and Stewardship

      TruexCullins is an integrated architecture and interior
      design firm providing planning, design, and project
      management services for regional, national, and
      international clients. For more than 50 years, our work has
      spanned education, workplace, residential, and hospitality
      environments, guided by the belief that design has the
      power to improve communities and enrich everyday
      experience.
      Education is a core focus of our practice. We work with
      public school districts, independent schools, colleges,
      universities, and international schools to develop plans
      and learning environments that support wellness, learning,
      and engagement.
      Our work ranges from district-wide planning and
      facility assessments to renovations, additions, campus
      improvements, and new construction. Many of our
      educational clients have worked with TruexCullins for
      years and, in some cases, decades, allowing us to support
      both immediate needs and long-term goals as schools
      evolve and communities change.
