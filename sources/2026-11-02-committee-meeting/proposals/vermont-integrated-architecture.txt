1 PROJECT UNDERSTANDING




The Harwood Unified Union School District (HUUSD) is seeking architecture and engineering
services to perform a comprehensive feasibility and planning study regarding the potential
consolidation of four elementary schools (Fayston, Moretown, Waitsfield, and Warren)
into one building at 193 Home Farm Way in Waitsfield. The decision to consolidate and/or
close schools has not yet been made. The intent of the study is to evaluate the feasibility
of a centralized elementary campus for the four schools in question and to thoroughly
compare that option with reasonable alternatives, considering relevant enrollment, program,
operational cost, transportation, and facility information. The results of the study will be
shared with the School Board and the public to inform future decision making.

Vermont Integrated Architecture, P.C. (VIA) has partnered with HMFH Architects, a nationally
recognized public school designer, and together VIA + HMFH are pleased to submit this proposal.
VIA + HMFH understand the most critical questions to be addressed with this study to be:

•   Will consolidating these schools enhance program opportunities for the collective student
    and faculty bodies?
•   What will it cost to consolidate the schools, and ultimately, will it save the district
    taxpayers money?
•   Can we build consensus within the community around a collective direction forward?
•   Is 193 Home Farm Way an appropriate site for a consolidated elementary school? What
    are the opportunities and constraints with this existing building and site?
•   What are the Vermont regulatory hurdles this project would face should it move forward?
•   Is it possible to consolidate with the highest standards of sustainability and energy
    efficiency? Can we do this in such a way that ultimately is good for the planet, builds
    resilience for our community, ensures a healthy learning and work environment, and
    ultimately saves money?

VIA + HMFH would be delighted to work with HUUSD and the Mad River Valley communities
involved to explore and answer these questions.

 2 PROJECT APPROACH
MULTI-SCHOOL PLANNING & CONSOLIDATION
Public school buildings are the core of every community and a great source of civic pride,
supporting students in their academic, social, and emotional development and offering valuable
district-wide resources. The VIA + HMFH partnership brings a design approach that is rooted
in community-focused, client-specific planning efforts. Together, our team can offer extensive
experience gained from a wealth of building assessments, site assessments, and feasibility
studies for public schools and other education and public projects in the region, including
Vermont.
HMFH has worked on over 125 public schools in New England, including 57 school planning
studies in the last 10 years, to examine existing school buildings and prepare cost-effective
solutions for meeting current and anticipated educational and facility needs. VIA, which now
includes Waitsfield-based Maclay Architects) has worked on hundreds of projects throughout
Vermont to navigate public funding processes, complex permitting situations, and to ensure the
energy efficiency, healthfulness, and overall, long-term sustainability of Vermont buildings and
their occupants.
Together our team offers:
   •   Extensive public elementary school experience.
   •   Building design and construction expertise.
   •   Deep understanding of Vermont permitting and regulatory processes.
   •   Local knowledge and expertise, and a stake in the outcome of this study.
   •   Competent estimating, financial analysis, and budget management skills.
   •   Commitment to sustainability and designing for a healthy, fossil-fuel-free future.
   •   Passion for designing spaces that are great places to learn, work, and play.
Our collective process considers the many interrelated components of a public school
system—educational programs, community values, physical spaces, finance, operations, and
environment, along with enrollment projection impacts. Building on the initial data collection
and investigation, the analysis phase ensures all parties have a collective knowledge of existing
parameters and can confidently participate in future decision-making. A thoughtful, inclusive,
and transparent outreach process of shared information and open dialogue will be critical in
building support among project stakeholders.

EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMMING & ENROLLMENT PLANNING
VIA + HMFH pride themselves on listening to our clients and facilitating an iterative process
resulting in outcomes that will be specific and uniquely tailored to HUUSD. The success of
an Educational Program depends on input, engagement, and collaboration with you and
the communities affected. Our process requires us to understand the existing educational
paradigm in the community, invite input, and discuss best practices in the design of 21st
century educational spaces.
VIA + HMFH will prepare and present project approaches that holistically address educational

buildings and available sites. The goal is to look with a broad brush at many possibilities and
establish a common understanding of district-wide goals and concerns such as balanced
enrollment, equity, resiliency, and responsible use of existing facilities and town funds.


COST ESTIMATING & LIFE CYCLE ANALYSIS FOR PUBLIC SCHOOLS
Public projects are a significant community investment and require wise and careful spending
of taxpayer money. Each dollar spent must contribute to the school’s educational mission and
have a positive effect on the overall HUUSD budget for years to come.
During planning, as VIA + HMFH work with HUUSD to establish the project budget, we will
prepare high-level cost estimates that allow the team to understand the up-front cost to
achieve the community’s aspirational goals and balance this with the realistic financial
situation of the district now and in the future.
Both VIA and HMFH are used to working with tight budgets. We welcome this challenge. As
one example, the Neighborhood House Charter School initially faced a significant obstacle:
the need for more program space than their budget could build. Because of HMFH’s in-house
educational programming expertise, the team was able to suggest an alternative scheme
of space utilization and scheduling, lowering the overall square footage significantly and
reducing the construction cost by 25%.
A thorough review of the whole life cycle for a project is key in understanding the initial costs
and long-term benefits of such a significant decision. Life cycle cost analyses are routinely
performed to determine the correct balance of building systems to maximize efficiency, often
resulting in significant funding and rebates. Our collective of experienced staff have helped
our clients make well-informed decisions and deliver successful projects that are responsive
to the environmental, social, economic, and educational needs of each community.

We call ourselves Vermont Integrated Architecture, or VIA, because we work with our clients,
consultants, and other project stakeholders to find a better way (‘VIA’) to conceive of buildings
and their surroundings. We take pride in our VERMONT sensibility and believe our work is
not about us, but our clients and the many other people who contribute to its design and
development, hence an INTEGRATED process. We have intentionally grown our firm to be a
collaborative and a laboratory for the advancement of ARCHITECTURE that is timeless, yet
reflective of its place. All of these values are rooted in our commitment to sustainability.

renovations, and new facilities. From childcare facilities to university campuses, VIA works with

ENGINEERING VENTURES, PC
Structural & Civil Enginering
Engineering Ventures, PC (EV) is an experienced structural and civil consulting engineer-
ing firm with offices in Burlington, VT, Lebanon, NH, and Saratoga Springs, NY. Our team
of qualified professionals and technicians provide a broad range of services to meet the
needs of our private and public clients in the northeastern US and beyond. We strive to
create sustainably built environments for our communities. This means implementing
environmentally friendly design practices and pursuing projects that nurture the public.

Civil Engineering and Permitting
Engineering Ventures’ civil team collaborates with clients to understand and fit the proj-
ect goals to each unique site. Our team is well versed in site evaluation and equipped to
handle often complex and challenging environmental considerations. Our project scopes
often include direct responsibility for Alteration of Terrain, Shoreland, and Sewer/Septic
permitting, and we often provide designs and permitting assistance for Wetlands permits.
We specialize in campus projects, infill development, and challenging sites, and we com-
bine traditional engineering practices with innovative technologies and creative solutions.
Our site designs focus on long term sustainable solutions with consideration for health,
safety, and the well-being of our shared environment. EV staff foster long-term working
relationships with clients and regulators to facilitate timely and predictable permit pro-
cesses for each project. Experienced EV staff provide quality control in developing con-
struction documents and construction phase services.

Structural Engineering
Engineering Ventures structural team collaborates with their clients to ensure that struc-
tural considerations are incorporated into designs at the beginning of the project. This
close cooperation translates into buildings that are efficient, effectively coordinated, and
cost effective. Services offered include building code compliance, new building system
structural design, historic building evaluation and restoration, load analysis and design,
rigid and braced frames for earthquake and wind, retaining walls, fire walls, trusses,
foundations, composite systems, and roofs. The professionals at Engineering Ventures are
well versed in heavy timber, lumber and manufactured wood, structural steel, light gauge
steel, reinforced concrete (placed, precast, and fabric formed), and masonry.

Schools K-12
Schools are an area of specialty for EV and the firm has been involved in most of the sub-
stantial Vermont-based school projects over the last two decades. This work includes ex-
pansions at CVU, Brattleboro, Enosburg, and Hanover (in the Dresden cross-state district).
Recent projects include Shelburne, Williston, South Burlington, and Warren Schools, as
well as projects for the Little Falls, Hoosick Falls, and Moriah School Districts
