The meeting was preceded by an optional Board Professional Development session, covering school finance and two-factor authentication.


Ashley Woods called the meeting to order at 6:04 pm.


Public Comment: 
Tommy Young of Duxbury, speaking as a Waitsfield educator, provided comments regarding the ability to offer more to elementary students if they were together in a Valley-wide school.


Louisa Wilson of Moretown asked for clarity regarding programming and financial details related to consolidation plans.


Jessie Williams of Moretown voiced her concerns regarding the potential closure of Moretown School.


Peter Langella of Moretown offered some comments regarding school consolidation not necessarily leading to more equity and asked for related details.


Kim Laidlaw of Fayston spoke of the benefits possible in a larger school setting, and asked Lisa to present related financial information in detail.


Approve Agenda:
A motion to approve the agenda passed unanimously.


Recognition of Teacher of the Year Finalist Pam Dow:
The Board recognized Pam for this honor, and she spoke of her work at Moretown School.


Discussion:
Budget - Lisa and Dr. Mike presented preliminary budget information, first reviewing information regarding reductions made in recent years, and then presenting an overview of three potential budget scenarios. Those included a budget cap threshold, consolidation of the four Valley elementary schools into two, and a merger of the middle schools. The combination of the two mergers outlined would provide for the over $3M reduction required to stay below the upcoming excess spending threshold.  The roadmap for school reconfiguration and consolidation through FY32 was outlined, as well as the budget development timeline for FY28.  More details were provided as Lisa, Dr. Mike, and other administrative staff answered questions from Board members. Board members asked that further information regarding the consolidation scenarios be presented at the next meeting, along with related transportation costs and times when those are able to be determined.


Early Literacy Programming - Chris Neville and Tommy Young gave a presentation explaining how literacy is taught in the District, and how technology use enhances that instruction, particularly in allowing for evaluation and providing differentiation in teaching.  Following the presentation, questions from Board members were addressed.


Board Reports: 
* Student Report - Students reported on the golf team’s success, a current Boosters challenge, Homecoming events, and an upcoming Battle of the Bands.
* Board Reports
   * Merger Study Committee - Cindy indicated that the committee had not yet met due to logistics, but that a meeting should take place soon.
   * Outreach Committee - Caitlin reported on planned School Board website changes, the recent Moretown budget presentation and plans for holding presentations in other towns, and recommendations for improvements to the Ongoing Question Document responses.  She also spoke of the formation of a strategic planning and advisory group, and requested that participants be suggested. Finally, she requested that the Board approve the slide deck which was created for use during budget presentations.  Also noted were Board meeting recaps and a reminder of the upcoming candidate forum being hosted by the Middle School.
