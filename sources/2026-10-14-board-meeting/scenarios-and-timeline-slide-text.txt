Draft Proposal for School Reconfiguration & Consolidation Roadmap
Multi-year operational transition plan from FY28 through FY32 aligning budget sustainability with school facility consolidation





PHASE 1 • FY28
Initial School Consolidation & New Configurations
Consolidation: 4 valley elementary ➞ 2 valley elementary |  CBMS ➞ HUMS
2 valley elementary: Grade PreK–4 (2 valley elementary PreK-4, BPS PreK-4)
CBMS: Becomes Crossett Brook Upper Elementary (CBUES) for Grades 5–6
HUMS: Becomes 7th & 8th grade center
PHASE 2 • FY30
New Location for Valley Elementary
2 Valley elementary move into new elementary facility; advancing 4th graders remain
➞ Valley Elementary reconfigures to preK–5
PHASE 3 • FY31
CB Grade Retention & Facility Preparation
CB maintains 5th & 6th grades (BPS students & valley 5th & 6th graders)
Facility Action: Begin renovation work on CB to accommodate BPS elementary students (isolating floor or wing renovations)
PHASE 4 • FY32
Relocation of BPS & Final Reconfiguration to be Determined
