==================== SLIDE 16 (slide16.xml) imgs=[]
  [TEXT] PROGRAMMATIC BENEFITS OF CONSOLIDATION
  [TEXT] What Scale Can Mean for Students
  [TEXT] A practical example: specialized reading instruction for students with IEPs
  [TEXT] IN SEPARATE SCHOOLS
  [TEXT] 1 student + 3 students
  [TEXT] School A: One student works one-on-one with a teacher because no appropriate peers are available.
         School B: Three students with similar reading needs work with a second teacher.
  [TEXT] IN A CONSOLIDATED SCHOOL
  [TEXT] 4 students, both teachers retained
  [TEXT] If reading levels and IEPs support grouping, one teacher teaches the four students together.
         The other teacher extends support to different students who cannot currently access that additional help in either building.
  [TEXT] WHY LEARNING WITH PEERS IS IMPORTANT
  [TEXT] One-on-one instruction is sometimes necessary, but it is not always the best setting. An appropriate small group lets students practice together, learn from one another, and build confidence and belonging.
  [TEXT] Each student’s IEP and instructional needs guide grouping and services.
